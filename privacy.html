Compliance & Governance

Privacy Policy

How Lend Partners protects, handles and manages your personal and credit information in strict compliance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

Credit Licence & Privacy Act Compliance

In this policy, ‘we’, ‘us’ and ‘our’ refers to Lend Partners Group Pty Ltd (ACN 690 636 581), an authorised corporate credit representative (Credit Representative No. 500558) of LMG Broker Services Pty Ltd (ACN 632 405 504, Australian Credit Licence 517192). This policy sets out our management of personal information under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

1. Our Commitment to Protect Your Privacy

We understand how important it is to protect your personal information. This document sets out our policy on how we collect, hold, use, and manage personal information we hold about borrowers and consumers, including personal information collected in connection with a credit application or credit facility.

Our commitment is to abide by the Australian Privacy Principles as set out in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and any other applicable Australian laws.

2. What is Personal Information?

When used in this privacy policy, the term “personal information” has the meaning given to it in the Privacy Act. In general terms, it is any information that can be used to personally identify you, including your name, address, telephone number, email address, date of birth, and profession or occupation. If the information we collect personally identifies you, or you are reasonably identifiable from it, the information will be considered personal information.

3. How We Collect Your Personal Information

If you deal with us directly, we generally collect personal information directly from you, such as in person, on the phone, or electronically. This includes your interaction with our website, online inquiry functionalities, loan questionnaires, and communication channels.

We may also collect your personal information in other ways, including:

  • From joint applicants, co-applicants, or guarantors / proposed guarantors;
  • From the loan applicant if you are a guarantor or proposed guarantor;
  • From your employer, real estate agent, conveyancer, accountant, solicitor, or other referees;
  • From third parties such as credit reporting bodies (Equifax, Experian, illion), lenders, government entities, and identity verification service providers; and
  • When legally required to do so in the provision of specific credit services.

We only collect credit reporting information and credit eligibility information from credit reporting bodies and credit providers if you expressly authorise us to do so in writing as part of providing credit assistance services.

4. What Personal Information We Collect and Hold

We may collect and hold the following types of personal information:

  • Identity information: Your name, date of birth, residential address, drivers licence, passport, and verification documents;
  • Contact details: Physical address, email address, telephone numbers;
  • Financial & credit information: Tax file numbers (handled strictly per TFN guidelines), bank account details, superannuation or insurance policies, credit reports, income, living expenses, assets, liabilities, and repayment history including any defaults;
  • Application context: Age and number of dependants, employment tenure, proof of earnings, business structures (ABN/ACN, trust deeds); and
  • Sensitive information: Where relevant to loan structuring, hardship, or insurance (e.g. professional memberships, or health information provided voluntarily).

5. Purpose of Collecting Your Personal Information

We collect, hold, use and disclose your personal information for:

  • Carrying on our business and providing credit assistance to you;
  • Managing our relationship and communicating effectively with you;
  • Assessing your eligibility for commercial, development, residential, business, or asset finance products;
  • Assisting you to prepare, structure and submit credit applications to panel lenders;
  • Conducting business processing, risk management, and administrative functions;
  • Meeting our legal and regulatory obligations under the National Consumer Credit Protection Act 2009 (NCCP Act), Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act 2006 (AML/CTF), and ASIC directives; and
  • Where permitted by law, direct marketing of financial insights and relevant services.

Consequences of Not Providing Information: If you do not provide us with the required information, we may not be able to provide our credit assistance services to you or submit your loan application to lenders.

6. To Whom We Disclose Personal Information

We may disclose your personal information:

  • To our mortgage aggregator and licensee, LMG Broker Services Pty Ltd (ACN 632 405 504, ACL 517192), through whom we submit applications and undergo compliance auditing;
  • To prospective or actual lenders, lessors, private funders, and mortgage insurers on our panel;
  • To industry bodies, valuers, employers, referees, and identity verification services;
  • To credit reporting bodies when authorised to obtain credit reports on your behalf;
  • To your professional advisers (accountants, lawyers, conveyancers, buyers agents);
  • To cloud software hosts and ICT infrastructure providers that assist us in operating our credit practice; and
  • To government authorities, law enforcement agencies, or regulators where required by Australian law.

7. Disclosure to Overseas Entities

Some of the cloud infrastructure, enterprise software, and service providers hosting or processing your personal information may be located outside Australia (such as New Zealand, the United States of America, or the Philippines). We take all reasonable steps to ensure that any overseas recipients do not breach Australian Privacy Principles regarding your personal information.

8. Automated Decision-Making & Software Systems

We use industry-standard software systems (including aggregator CRM and credit assessment tools) to support us in filtering and identifying suitable lending products based on lender policy and eligibility criteria. Any software used is explicitly a tool to assist analysis — human credit specialists, and not an automated algorithm, make all final credit recommendations and decisions.

9. Direct Marketing & Opt-Out

From time to time, we may send you insights about market movements, interest rates, or relevant credit opportunities. You may opt out of receiving direct marketing at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in our emails, emailing hello@lendpartners.com.au, or calling 1300 221 888. We do not charge any fee for processing opt-out requests.

10. Accessing and Correcting Your Personal Information

You have rights under the Privacy Act to request access to the personal information we hold about you, free of charge:

  • Access: We will respond to your request within 30 days. If we deny access in specific circumstances permitted by law (e.g. legal proceedings), an explanation will be provided.
  • Correction: If any information is incorrect, inaccurate, or outdated, you may request a correction. We will acknowledge within 7 days and provide details of correction within 30 days.

11. Government Identifiers & Anonymity

If we collect government identifiers (such as Tax File Numbers), we do not use or disclose this information other than as authorised by law. We never adopt any government identifier as our internal identifier of you. Where lawful and practicable, you may interact with us anonymously or using a pseudonym for general, non-transactional inquiries.

12. Security and Storage

We take all reasonable steps to store your personal information in secure electronic environments with multi-factor authentication, enterprise firewalls, and encryption protocols. When personal information is no longer needed and legally permissible, we securely destroy or de-identify it.

13. Privacy Complaints & OAIC Escalation

If you have any complaint about our compliance with the Privacy Act, please contact our Privacy Officer in writing. We will acknowledge your complaint within 7 days and provide a formal decision within 30 days.
