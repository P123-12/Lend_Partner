Compliance & Governance

Privacy Policy

How Lend Partners protects, handles and manages your personal and credit information in strict compliance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

Credit Licence Disclosure

Lend Partners Group Pty Ltd (ABN 45 632 405 504) is an authorised credit representative (Corporate Credit Representative No. 517792) of LMG Broker Services Pty Ltd ACN 632 405 504 Australian Credit Licence 517192. Our Privacy Policy aligns with LMG's privacy practices.

1. Commitment to Privacy

We respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding the personal and financial information you entrust to us. This policy outlines how we collect, hold, use and disclose your personal and credit-related information in connection with credit activities, lending assessment and brokerage services.

2. Types of Information We Collect

To provide credit assistance and secure suitable finance structures, we collect:

  • Identification information: Full name, date of birth, residential address, drivers licence, passport details.
  • Financial position: Income records, payslips, tax returns, financial statements, bank accounts, existing loans, and assets/liabilities.
  • Credit information: Credit history, credit reporting data from reporting bodies (Equifax, Experian, illion), defaults, repayment history, and court proceedings.
  • Entity details: Company names, ABN/ACN, trust deeds, partnership agreements, and beneficial ownership structures.

3. How We Collect Information

We primarily collect information directly from you through loan application forms, meetings, telephone conversations, emails, and website enquiry submissions. We may also collect information from third parties where authorised, such as accountants, financial planners, employers, real estate agents, solicitors, and credit reporting agencies.

4. Purposes of Collection, Use and Disclosure

We collect and use your personal information to:

  • Assess your eligibility and borrowing capacity for residential, commercial, development, or asset finance.
  • Source, structure and negotiate lending solutions across our accredited panel of 60+ banks and specialist lenders.
  • Submit credit applications to chosen lenders and manage approvals through to settlement.
  • Comply with regulatory obligations under the National Consumer Credit Protection Act 2009 (NCCP Act), Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act 2006 (AML/CTF), and responsible lending guidelines.

5. Disclosure of Personal Information

We may disclose your information to:

  • Lenders, banks and private credit providers evaluating your credit application.
  • Our aggregator, LMG Broker Services Pty Ltd (ACN 632 405 504, ACL 517192), for compliance auditing and software processing.
  • Credit reporting bodies when conducting credit enquiries on your behalf with your consent.
  • Your designated professional advisers (accountant, solicitor, conveyancer).
  • Government authorities, law enforcement, or regulatory bodies where required by Australian law.

6. Security and Storage

We maintain strict digital and physical safeguards to prevent loss, misuse, unauthorised access, modification or disclosure. Electronic records are stored in enterprise-grade, encrypted cloud databases located in secure Australian data centres.

7. Access and Correction

You have the right to request access to personal information we hold about you and to request corrections if you believe it is inaccurate or incomplete. Requests can be submitted to our Privacy Officer.
