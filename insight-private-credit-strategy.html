When is Private Credit the Right Tool for Your Move?

A strategic evaluation of non-bank lenders: understanding when speed, covenant flexibility, and bespoke terms outweigh headline pricing.

Read Full Analysis ↓
Published: 21.05.26 · 5 min read · Private Credit Desk

Key Strategic Takeaways

  • Execution Velocity: Private credit can settle in 5 to 10 business days compared to 6 to 12 weeks for tier-1 institutional banks.
  • Opportunity Cost vs Margin: Paying a higher margin for 12 months is often substantially cheaper than missing a discounted off-market acquisition.
  • Pre-Planned Exit Strategy: Every private debt facility must incorporate an unambiguous take-out plan back into traditional institutional banking.

1. The Rise of Private Debt in Australia

Over the last five years, Australia’s private credit market has evolved from a niche alternative into an indispensable component of commercial lending architecture. Stricter APRA regulatory capital requirements on traditional trading banks have caused mainstream institutions to retreat from non-standard transactions, complex entity structures, and rapid settlement timelines.

Private debt funds, family offices, and wholesale credit managers have stepped into this void, offering bespoke lending solutions where flexibility and certainty of execution take precedence over rigid box-ticking.

2. When Traditional Banks Fall Short

Borrowers typically turn to private credit not because their fundamentals are flawed, but because their transactional requirements conflict with retail banking guidelines:

  • Time-Critical Acquisitions: Securing an off-market commercial property or distressed asset requiring unconditional settlement within 14 business days.
  • Non-Standard Financial Track Records: High-net-worth entrepreneurs, property developers, or business owners whose recent tax returns reflect strategic capital deductions rather than normalized earnings.
  • Transitional Property Assets: Commercial buildings undergoing major repositioning, tenant lease renegotiation, or capital improvements where current cashflow does not yet satisfy standard bank ICR covenants.
  • Equity Release for Urgent Liquidity: Releasing capital from real estate assets to seize immediate corporate mergers, acquisitions, or inventory opportunities.

Evaluating the Total Cost of Capital

A borrower may hesitate at a private credit interest rate of 9.5% compared to a bank rate of 6.5%. However, if the 9.5% facility enables an acquisition at an 18% discount to valuation with zero pre-sale requirements, the economic return far outstrips the incremental borrowing margin over a 12-month period.

3. Key Structuring Pillars of Private Facilities

Private credit facilities are tailored to borrower cashflows. Common structural options include:

  1. Capitalised Interest: Allowing interest payments to be rolled into the loan facility up to a maximum LVR, eliminating monthly cash serviceability pressure during asset repositioning.
  2. Flexible Loan Terms: Short to medium-term facilities ranging from 6 to 24 months with transparent pre-payment clauses.
  3. Asset-Backed Underwriting: Focus on underlying real estate asset quality, valuation metrics, and strategic location rather than historic personal tax returns.

4. The Golden Rule: Discipline Around Your Exit Strategy

Private credit is by design a transitional bridge, not a permanent home. At Lend Partners, we mandate that every private credit facility be established with an executable, multi-path exit strategy—whether through institutional bank refinance, completed asset sale, or secondary capitalization. Our credit desk works with you from day one to architect both the entry and the seamless exit.
