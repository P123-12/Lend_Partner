Lending Architecture

Structure first. Interest rate second.

Most retail mortgage brokers focus purely on interest rates. However, an improperly structured home loan can compromise tax deductibility, cross-collateralise disparate assets, and choke future borrowing capacity. We ensure your debt supports the life you are actively building.

Confidential, high-touch mortgage advisory for prime family residences. Handling bridging finance, simultaneous settlements, and private banking terms.

Unbundling cross-collateralised investment properties into standalone facilities with separate lenders, mitigating contagion risk and restoring equity control.

Specialist assessment of complex financial statements, add-backs, retained corporate earnings, and multiple beneficiary distribution channels.

Navigating strict compliance under the Superannuation Industry (Supervision) Act (SIS Act) to acquire high-grade residential investments within super.
