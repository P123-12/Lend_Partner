2. Our Internal Dispute Resolution (IDR) Process & Timeframes

When we receive a complaint, we will attempt to resolve it promptly:

  • Receipt Acknowledgement: We will provide a written acknowledgement of receipt of the complaint to you within 24 hours (1 business day) or as soon as practicable, unless the complaint is resolved in the meantime.
  • Standard Resolution Timeframe: We will ensure that a final response is given to you as soon as possible, but strictly within thirty (30) calendar days of receipt of the complaint.
  • Urgent Disputes & Hardship: For certain types of complaints involving “default notices” or urgent disputes such as “applications for financial hardship”, a final response must be provided within twenty-one (21) calendar days.
  • Complaints Involving Third Parties: If we are unable to deal with the complaint as it relates to a third party (for example, a lender or valuer), we may ask you to contact the relevant third party directly, or assist you in routing the inquiry.
  • Delays: In cases where your complaint will take longer than 30 days to resolve due to complexity, we will notify you in writing with the reasons for the delay and inform you of your right to refer the complaint to the Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA).

3. External Dispute Resolution (EDR) Scheme — AFCA

If we do not reach an agreement on your complaint, you may refer the complaint to an ASIC-approved External Dispute Resolution (EDR) Scheme. Our external dispute resolution provider is the Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA).

Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA)

Online: www.afca.org.au

Email: info@afca.org.au

Telephone: 1800 931 678 (toll free)

Mail: GPO Box 3, Melbourne VIC 3001

External dispute resolution is a free service established to provide you with an independent mechanism to resolve specific complaints. You may refer the matter to AFCA at any time, but if our internal process is still in progress, they may request that our internal processes be completed before considering the matter further.
