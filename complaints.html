Complaints & Feedback

We are dedicated to providing excellent service. If something does not meet your expectations, we have a clear, transparent and prompt resolution process.

Regulatory Commitment

Lend Partners operates in strict compliance with ASIC Regulatory Guide 271 (Internal Dispute Resolution). We are an authorised representative (CRN 517792) of LMG Broker Services Pty Ltd ACN 632 405 504 Australian Credit Licence 517192.

1. Our Dispute Resolution Philosophy

We take all client feedback seriously. Whether you have a suggestion, question, or complaint regarding our credit assistance, staff conduct, or loan handling, we are committed to resolving the issue fairly, objectively and efficiently at no charge to you.

2. Step 1: Internal Dispute Resolution (IDR)

If you have a complaint, please reach out directly to your credit adviser or contact our Complaints Resolution Team:

Complaints Officer — Lend Partners Group Pty Ltd

Email: complaints@lendpartners.com.au

Phone: 1300 221 888

Address: Level 28, 101 Collins Street, Melbourne VIC 3000

Our Commitment:

  • We will acknowledge receipt of your complaint within 1 business day.
  • We will thoroughly investigate the circumstances and provide a comprehensive written response within 30 calendar days.
  • If your complaint relates to financial hardship, default notices or postponement of enforcement, we will respond within 21 calendar days.

3. Step 2: External Dispute Resolution (AFCA)

If you are not satisfied with our final internal response, or if your complaint has not been resolved within 30 days, you have the right to lodge your complaint with the Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA).

AFCA is an external financial services dispute resolution scheme available to consumers at no charge:

Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA)

Website: www.afca.org.au

Email: info@afca.org.au

Phone: 1800 931 678
