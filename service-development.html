Structured Facilities

Unlocking liquidity at every stage of development.

Development finance requires an intimate knowledge of QS reports, builder warranties, tripartite agreements, and progressive drawdown schedules. We structure the capital stack to protect developer equity.

Competitive senior construction facilities funded by major Australian banks and debt funds. Structured with stage drawdowns against quantity surveyor certifications.

Subordinated capital bridging the gap between senior debt and sponsor equity, enabling developers to pursue concurrent projects without over-leveraging equity reserves.

Post-completion facilities that repay the construction facility, allowing developers to execute an orderly, unhurried sales campaign for unsold apartments or townhouses.

Strategic acquisition capital for raw land and approved sites, structured to accommodate rezoning timelines and civil works delivery.
